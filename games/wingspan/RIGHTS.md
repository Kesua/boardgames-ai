Wingspan © Stonemaier Games (designer: Elizabeth Hargrave). This repository is an unofficial fan research project.
A licensed digital version of Wingspan exists; nothing here plays the game or competes with it.

What is here: bird names (for identification), card numbers and powers as structured data with own-words
descriptions generated from that data, rules summarised in own words (rules.md), engine code, trained models and
statistics. What is not here: rulebooks, card art, logos, copied card or rules text.
