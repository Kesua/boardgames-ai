Through the Ages: A New Story of Civilization © Czech Games Edition (designer: Vlaada Chvátil). This repository is an
unofficial fan research project. A licensed digital version exists (the official CGE app); nothing here plays the game
or competes with it.

What is here: card names (for identification), card numbers and effects as structured data with own-words
descriptions, rules summarised in own words (rules.md), engine code, trained models and statistics.
What is not here: rulebooks, card art, logos, copied card or rules text.
